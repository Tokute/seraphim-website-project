Requirements for Joining the Seraphim Guild

*Before continuing with the application process, please ensure you meet the following requirements:

Primary Requirements

  • Max Level Character: Level 99
  • Completed Professor Pho’s Guide until Varinimyr
  • Obtained Varinimyr Raid Weapon
  • Discord Account (For Guild Communication)
  • Combat Power > 1.5 Million, Defense > 250k

Highly Prioritized Characters:

  • Elsword: Genesis (PVP)
  • Raven: Rage Hearts (PVP)
  • Eve: Code: Sariel (PVE, DPS)
  • Elesis: Empire Sword (PVE, Sub-DPS)
  • Chung: Dius Aer (PVE, Healer)
  • Ara: Surya (PVE, Healer)
  • Noah: Celestia (PVE, Buffer)

Guild Rules

  1. Be Respectful and Courteous: Treat all guild members with respect. Harassment, toxicity, hate speech, or drama in guild chat or Discord will not be tolerated.
  2. Activity and Inactivity: Members are expected to stay active. If you plan to be away from the game for an extended period (more than 2 weeks), please notify an officer or the guild leader to avoid being removed during routine cleanups.
  3. Participation in Guild Features: Try to contribute to guild growth by participating in guild daily/weekly requests, checking the guild farm/garden system regularly, and utilizing shared resources responsibly.
  4. Raid and Endgame Etiquette: When joining guild raid parties, communicate effectively, come prepared with proper gear/consumables, and be patient with newer members learning mechanics.
  5. Guild Storage Etiquette: Only take items from the Guild Storage if you genuinely need them. Do not empty the storage for personal profit, and consider donating back when you have spare resources.
  6. Discord Presence: Joining our Discord server is heavily encouraged (or required) for important announcements, raid coordination, and farm notification reminders.
