Requirements for Joining the Seraphim Guild

*Before continuing with the application process, please ensure you meet the following requirements:

  • Max Level Character: Level 99
  • Completed Professor Pho’s Guide until Varinimyr
  • Obtained Varinimyr Raid Weapon
  • Discord Account (For Guild Communication)
  • Combat Power > 1.5 Million, Defense > 250k

Highly Prioritized Characters:

  • Elsword: Genesis (PVP)
  • Raven: Rage Hearts (PVP)
  • Eve: Code: Sariel (PVE, DPS)
  • Elesis: Empire Sword (PVE, Sub-DPS)
  • Chung: Dius Aer (PVE, Healer)
  • Ara: Surya (PVE, Healer)
  • Noah: Celestia (PVE, Buffer)

Proceed to the Recruitment Form page to submit your application.
