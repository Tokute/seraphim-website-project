Welcome to the Seraphim Guild of Elsword!

The Seraphim Guild is a guild active on both North America and International servers and provides reliable assistance and raids to its guild members. Seraphim was founded by our glorious leader _Xzenith5.

Seraphim’s mission is to bring support to new players and guide them on how to strive in Elsword’s harsh endgame environment. Seraphim’s vision is to be the first guild that comes into mind when it comes to raid mastery, high combat power, and domination over Elsword’s content.

If you are interested in getting stronger or helping people get stronger with you, join us now!

Why Joining Seraphim will help you in Elsword

Guild Storage
Guild Storage is a feature that allows guild members to store items in a shared storage space. This can be useful for sharing items with other members, or for storing items that you don't need right away.
Guild Skills
Guild Skills are special abilities that can be unlocked by the guild as a whole. These skills can provide benefits to all guild members, such as increased damage or reduced cooldowns. Seraphim constantly maintains high skill levels to ensure our members have the best possible support.
Support from Fellow Guild Members
Here in Seraphim, we promote and uphold camaraderie among our members, ensuring that everyone feels supported and valued. When it comes to raids, suggestions, build ideas, or questions, you'll always have a supportive community to turn to.
Guild Farm Notification System
Our guild integrates a notification system to remind members about the crops that they have planted in the guild garden. This is made possible through discord bots by logging in the crop you planted.
